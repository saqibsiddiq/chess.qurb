Product

Everything you need to improve

Full-strength Stockfish under the hood, not a throttled cloud engine.

Every move, classified

Chesy scores each position, compares what you played against the engine's best line, and labels the move the way you already read a review, then tells you plainly what changed.

Real Stockfish, locally

Full-strength Stockfish runs on your own machine over UCI. No request caps, no queue, no watered-down cloud engine.

Private by construction

No accounts, no telemetry, no network calls at runtime. Your games never leave the machine you played them on.

How it works

Three steps to a full review

From raw PGN to a move-by-move breakdown in seconds.

01

Load your PGN

Paste the text or open a file. Chesy reads it straight away. No import wizard, no format wrangling.

02

Stockfish analyses

Every position gets a deep local evaluation: centipawn scores, best lines and per-side accuracy.

03

Review and improve

Walk the game move by move with colour-coded labels and plain-English explanations of what changed.
